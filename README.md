# AGRIOPTI-
Farmers are facing real challenges with fragmented digital services, complex 
technical interfaces, limited access to farms, and no integrated intelligent support 
systems to help them and their agricultural industry partners with their farming 
decisions. To address these challenges, AgriOpti - An AI Driven Multilingual 
Platform for Sustainable Farming has been developed to provide a single platform 
that uses an integrated AI-assisted agricultural ecosystem to simplify agricultural 
decision-making through intelligent workflows that provide access to different 
languages and provide real-time analytical support. The AgriOpti system utilizes and 
integrates many machine learning and deep learning modules to provide growers with 
services such as crop recommendations, plant disease detections, crop yield 
predictions, AI-based chatbot assistance for agriculture, analysis of real-time aerial 
land, fertilization recommendations, weather predictions, and customized dashboards 
grouped by user roles. The  AgriOpti system was developed using the following 
technologies: React.js, Node.js, Python, Flask, Firebase, within an integrated 
architecture using Gemini API, Weather APIs, and voice recognition and speech 
synthesis. The model testing of different machine learning and deep learning 
algorithms for the AgriOpti system (Naive Bayes, EfficientNet-B0, Random Forest, 
Inception V1, etc.) were conducted based on their prediction accuracy, computation 
efficiency, and ability to infer real-time results for agricultural decision-making. The 
overall design of the AgriOpti system integrates and combines users from the front 
end, orchestration of the back end, processing of data in AI, cloud services for 
increasing speed and analytical reporting, as well as the integration of third party 
APIs to provide a flexible platform to meet the needs of farmers, retailers, and 
government to provide scalable solutions that can improve the agriculture sector 
through AI's potential.
